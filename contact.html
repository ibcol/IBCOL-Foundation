IBCOL Foundation (International Blockchain Olympiad)

IBCOL Foundation oversees education and competition in solution architecture and engineering design since 2017, especially via the International Blockchain Olympiad challenging students and recent graduates to practically apply blockchain and trust technologies from first principles to real-world challenges. IDSOL (International Data Science Olympiad) is the sister competition of IBCOL.
